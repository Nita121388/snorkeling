Slow monday. tier the greys — how do I keep the cast shadow a hair darker than the paper?
Answer
Use four values: paper · bone · soot · ink. Keep the cast shadow one notch below the object — its edge softer than the outline, never darker than the line. Underprint the shadow with a half-tone screen, not a flat fill.
